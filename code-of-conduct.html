Rules

Code of Conduct

WHYBE'26 is a space to build, learn and compete in good faith. These rules keep it fair, safe and respectful for everyone — read them before you register.

Important

The ₹80 registration fee per team is non-refundable, including in cases of disqualification or no-shows.

1. Eligibility & Teams

  • Teams must have exactly 4 members registered before the deadline.
  • Every team member must complete registration and pay the ₹80 entry fee.
  • Substituting or adding members after registration closes is not permitted.

2. Fair Use of AI Tools

  • WHYBE'26 is an AI-driven hackathon — using AI copilots, agents and models to build is expected and encouraged.
  • Teams must be able to explain how their solution works and what role AI played in building it.
  • Submitting a fully pre-built or previously published project as new work is not allowed.

3. Code of Behavior

  • Treat organizers, mentors, judges and fellow participants with respect.
  • Harassment, discrimination or abusive behavior of any kind will not be tolerated.
  • Plagiarism of code, design or ideas from other teams or external sources without credit is prohibited.

4. Judging Criteria

  • Innovation and originality of the idea.
  • Technical execution and completeness of the build.
  • Thoughtful, well-explained use of AI.
  • Clarity and quality of the final presentation.

5. Disqualification

Teams may be disqualified for plagiarism, harassment, violating these rules, or submitting after the deadline. Disqualification forfeits the entry fee and any leaderboard standing.

6. Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness a violation of this code of conduct, report it to organizers immediately at technocrats@gsv.ac.in or via our Instagram.
